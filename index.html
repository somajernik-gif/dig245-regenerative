Concept

A Day by Chance generates an imaginary forecast and a simple activity inspired by it. Visitors choose a season, then generate different combinations of weather, temperature, and suggestions.

The season sets the rules, while random selections make each result unpredictable. The project encourages visitors to pause and imagine a different way to spend part of their day.

Rules and Randomness

  • Each season has a temperature range and a list of possible weather conditions.
  • In this project, snowy forecasts use temperatures between 20°F and 32°F.
  • Activities are selected from lists that match the sky condition.
  • Each click makes new selections, although some combinations may repeat.

Made for inspiration, not actual weather predictions.
